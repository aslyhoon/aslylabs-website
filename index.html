Our Products

Two products, one goal: use AI to solve everyday problems for Indian students and young professionals.

🎤

AI Mock Interviewer

A practice platform that conducts realistic mock interviews and gives structured, actionable feedback — so students and job seekers walk into real interviews prepared, not guessing.

  • Interview sessions by mode (HR / technical), difficulty and topic
  • Answer evaluation with structured feedback on every response
  • Built-in code practice with live code execution
  • Session history so progress is visible over time
  • Built for placement season at Indian colleges
● Live nowFastAPI · React · PostgreSQL

💰

Smart Expense Tracker

A full-stack personal finance app that tracks spending and uses machine learning to forecast next month's expenses — before the money runs out.

  • ML-based expense forecasting from your own spending history
  • Category insights, budgets and monthly summaries
  • Microservice architecture: Spring Boot backend + FastAPI ML service
  • Secure accounts with a relational PostgreSQL data layer
  • Public cloud launch in progress

About AslyLabs

AslyLabs was founded in 2026 in Greater Noida, India. We are a bootstrapped, independent software studio with AI at the core of everything we ship — from interview simulation to financial forecasting.

Our users are students preparing for their first job and young professionals managing their first salary. We build for them first: practical tools, honest pricing, no bloat.
